About

Most AI portfolios start with the technology. I start with the problem. My job is to figure out what's actually slowing a business down, then build the right fix — sometimes that's an AI Agent that handles conversations or decisions on its own, sometimes it's an automated workflow connecting the tools already in use, and sometimes it's a custom machine learning model trained on specific data.

I don't lock into one method, because the problem shouldn't have to fit a fixed toolkit. I understand the problem, design the solution, build it end-to-end, and integrate it into how the business already works — so it gets used, not just demoed.
